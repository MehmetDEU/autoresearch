# Literature review notes (reminders)

Use these when writing the Introduction / Literature Review.

## Term choice
- **Main term:** translanguaging (tightly defined for this study)
- **Optional micro-term:** code-switching (moment-to-moment shifts)
- **Avoid as primary:** code-shifting

## Working definition for THIS paper
Translanguaging = flexible use of Turkish alongside English in EMI classrooms to manage comprehension and content delivery — **descriptive of practice**, not necessarily empowering pedagogy.

Separate:
- **What** → translanguaging practice  
- **Why** → QUAL themes. Two motives, kept separate:
  - lecturer’s own limited English (compensatory; **not** Golem)
  - **Golem effect**: low expectancy of student English → translanguaging to secure content → less spoken English → oral–aural attrition (Babad, Inbar, & Rosenthal, 1982)

## Li Wei — purposes / compensatory?
- Li Wei allows **strategic and communicative functions**; translanguaging is purposeful meaning-making.
- It is **not** defined as a classic SLA “compensatory strategy,” but instances **can** be compensatory in motive.
- Paper wording: practice = translanguaging. Motives from data = (A) lecturer’s own English, (B) Golem underrating plus content security because Turkish is the shared L1.
- Do **not** reduce translanguaging to deficit-only, and do **not** call motive A a Golem effect. Report compensatory motives when participants say so.
- Lecturer excerpts: they are expected not to reflect on their own spoken English, and instead to point to a passed YDS / YÖKDİL (reading-based multiple choice; no Listening or Speaking). That excuse is context for motive A. Motive B (students’ low proficiency) remains the Golem link.

## Link to our RQs / findings story
- QUANT: L+S decline; R/W hold (EAP / written discourse vs sparse spoken EMI interaction).
- QUAL chain to protect in the discussion: lecturers presuppose weak student English (Golem) → frequent translanguaging / L1 as the safe way to keep content intact, especially with a shared Turkish L1 → four years of reduced verbal English → Listening and Speaking attrition. A separate, non-Golem path is lecturers compensating for their own English.
- Reading and Writing holding is part of the claim: the expectancy damages the skills that need spoken English, not the written coursework.
