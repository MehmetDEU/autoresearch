# Variable codebook

All scores are **synthetic**. Scale notes below match the institutional policy modelled in the workbook.

| Variable | Scale | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Student_ID | ID | Completer who sat both tests |
| Lecturer_ID | L01–L12 | Content lecturer (10 students each). Expectancy is a lecturer-held variable. |
| Major | 3 levels | Mechanical / Chemical / Electrical-Electronics Engineering (n = 40 each) |
| Gender | 2 levels | 82 male (68.3%), 38 female |
| Age_at_pre_PYP_exit | 18–21 | Age at Preparatory Year Programme exit test |
| Age_at_post_graduation | 22–25 | Age at the parallel graduation-year form |
| Pre_Listening / Reading / Writing / Speaking / Overall | 0–100 | PYP-exit institutional test. Overall = unweighted mean of four skills. Every Overall ≥ 60 (B1 pass). |
| Post_* | 0–100 | Parallel form four years later |
| Decline_* | points | Pre − Post. Positive = lower score at graduation |
| Lecturer_Est_English | 0–100 | Content lecturer’s estimate of that student’s English |
| Underrating_Gap | points | Pre_Overall − Lecturer_Est_English. Positive = underestimated (**Golem inaccuracy**) |
| TL_percent | 0–100 | Student estimate of content-course time in Turkish (**treatment**) |
| PU1–PU5, PU_mean | 1–5 | Perceived underrating |
| TL1–TL5, TL_mean | 1–5 | Translanguaging frequency Likert |
| EAP1–EAP5, EAP_mean | 1–5 | Lecturer’s own EAP limitation (**not Golem**; control) |
| WTC1–WTC5, WTC_mean | 1–5 | Willingness to communicate in English |
| SE1–SE5, SE_mean | 1–5 | English self-efficacy |
| Pre_L_S1–S4, Pre_R_S1–S4 | 0–25 | Listening/Reading sections for Cronbach’s α / McDonald’s ω |
| Post_L_S*, Post_R_S* | 0–25 | Parallel post sections |
| IELTS_subsample | Yes/No | Sat official ielts.org Academic practice materials at PYP exit (n = 36) |

Writing/Speaking rater sheets: each criterion is scored by Expert_Author, PYP_Instructor_A, and PYP_Instructor_B. Official skill score = mean of the three raters.

IELTS bands are 0–9 in 0.5 steps. Mapping used in the generator: band ≈ 5.0 + (institutional − 60) × 0.05, plus small error.
